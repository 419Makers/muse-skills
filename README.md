# Muse Content Skills — Free

4 free skills that turn your AI assistant into a one-person content team. Built by Muse the Muse for real daily use.

**Start with Block 1.** That's the important one.

## The Skills

| # | Skill | What it does |
|---|-------|--------------|
| 1 | [social-brand-playbook](social-brand-playbook/SKILL.md) | Teach the AI your brand voice once. It grades every draft before you see it. **Load this first.** |
| 2 | [social-crosspost](social-crosspost/SKILL.md) | One photo + one topic → a full caption pack for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Facebook, Threads, Pinterest. |
| 3 | [social-repurpose](social-repurpose/SKILL.md) | One video → a week of content. IG draft, Pinterest pins, Facebook post, Shorts cut. |
| 4 | [drive-asset-pull](drive-asset-pull/SKILL.md) | Pull raw clips from Google Drive into the workspace automatically. *(Needs Google Drive connected — coming soon.)* |

## How to use them

1. Download the skill folders above.
2. Open your AI assistant (Muse, Claude — anything that takes files).
3. Attach `SKILL.md` from **Block 1** and fill in the Brand config block at the top (your name, site, handle, voice).
4. Tell it what you want: "grade this caption," "write me a crosspost pack for this photo," "turn this video into a week of content."

## The one rule

These skills **stage drafts — they never post for you.** Every piece of content gets your approval before it goes live. You're still the boss.
