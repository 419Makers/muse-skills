# Social Repurpose

## Purpose
Multiply every filming session and every launch: one input becomes a week of platform-ready drafts.

## Workflow
1. Load the `social-brand-playbook` skill first — voice, platform, audio, and imagery rules plus the grader apply to every derivative.
2. Ingest the source: either raw video file(s) (note duration, key moments, the best 3–5 second hooks) or a product launch (name, price, link, 2–3 selling points — all sourced, never invented).
3. Plan the week's derivatives (default set; trim to fit the source):
   - 1 Instagram draft (image or clip + caption with link-in-bio CTA)
   - 2–3 Pinterest pins (distinct angles: product close-up, process shot, lifestyle/use case; each with title, description, destination URL)
   - 1 Facebook post (direct URL in body)
   - 1 YouTube Shorts cut suggestion (which 30–50s segment to cut, plus title and description with direct URL)
   - Optional: 1 TikTok cut, if its hook differs from the Shorts cut (native-audio note)
4. Draft every caption, title, and description per the playbook. Note the audio plan for each video derivative — never silent.
5. Grade every derivative with the playbook grader. Fix hard fails.
6. Stage the full week as drafts (see Output Contract) and report per-item verdicts. A human approves each item before anything is scheduled. Never auto-post.

## Output Contract
A content plan listing each derivative: platform, format, source segment or angle, caption/title text, hashtags, link (URL or `link-in-bio`), audio plan (video), media reference, grader verdict, and status `draft`. Delivered as one markdown file.

## Operating Rules
1. Derivatives must be genuinely distinct angles — not the same caption copy-pasted across platforms.
2. Every video derivative ships with an audio plan. A derivative with no audio path is cut from the plan, not staged silent.
3. Source facts only: no invented prices, claims, or timelines.
4. This skill stages drafts. Scheduling and publishing are human-approved follow-ups.
